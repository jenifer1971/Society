HOW TO USE THIS DEMO IN MIROFISH
=================================

STEP 1 — SEEDS OF REALITY (upload the 7 .txt files in /seeds/)
Upload all files in the seeds/ folder as your knowledge base documents.
These give the simulation its factual grounding in real automotive market dynamics.

  01_automotive_market_overview.txt  — industry-wide context 2023-2026
  02_bosch.txt                       — Robert Bosch GmbH profile & news
  03_valeo.txt                       — Valeo SE profile & news
  04_byd.txt                         — BYD Company profile & news
  05_bmw.txt                         — BMW Group profile & news
  06_continental.txt                 — Continental AG profile & news
  07_partnerships_and_deals.txt      — cross-industry deals & competitive moves

STEP 2 — MOCK PROMPT (copy the text from mock_prompt.txt)
Paste the contents of mock_prompt.txt as your simulation requirement.
This defines the scenario, agents, and questions the simulation will explore.

EXPECTED SIMULATION BEHAVIOR
The simulation will generate agents representing executives, analysts, journalists,
union leaders, and policymakers debating the six strategic announcements outlined
in the mock prompt. The knowledge graph built from the seed documents will ground
agent behavior in real company histories, partnerships, and competitive dynamics.
