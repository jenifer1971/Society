BYD COMPANY LIMITED — COMPANY PROFILE AND NEWS 2023–2026
=========================================================

COMPANY OVERVIEW

BYD Company Limited (Build Your Dreams) is a Chinese multinational conglomerate headquartered in Shenzhen, Guangdong Province, China. Founded in 1995 by Wang Chuanfu as a battery manufacturer, BYD has grown into the world's largest manufacturer of new energy vehicles (NEV) and one of the largest battery producers globally. The company is dual-listed on the Hong Kong Stock Exchange (1211.HK) and Shenzhen Stock Exchange (002594.SZ). Warren Buffett's Berkshire Hathaway is a significant shareholder.

BYD's automotive operations span passenger cars, commercial vehicles, buses, and rail transit systems. The company is uniquely vertically integrated, manufacturing its own batteries, semiconductors (through BYD Semiconductor), electric motors, power electronics, and even producing its own steel. This integration provides cost advantages and supply chain security that most global competitors cannot replicate.

2023: WORLD'S TOP EV SELLER

BYD delivered 1.57 million pure battery electric vehicles (BEV) and 1.44 million plug-in hybrid electric vehicles (PHEV) in 2023, for a total of 3.02 million NEV deliveries. In Q4 2023, BYD surpassed Tesla in quarterly pure BEV deliveries, becoming the world's largest EV manufacturer by volume for that quarter.

Annual revenue reached approximately RMB 602 billion (approximately $85 billion), representing 43% year-on-year growth. Net profit surged to approximately RMB 30 billion, up from RMB 16 billion in 2022.

BYD's key models in 2023:
- BYD Seagull: entry-level BEV priced from RMB 73,800 (~$10,000), targeting mass-market urban mobility
- BYD Seal: mid-size BEV sedan competing with Tesla Model 3, praised for driving dynamics
- BYD Han: flagship large sedan, popular with fleet and executive buyers
- BYD Atto 3 (Yuan Plus): compact SUV, BYD's primary export vehicle
- Denza D9: premium MPV sold through BYD's luxury Denza sub-brand
- Yangwang U8: ultra-premium off-road SUV priced above RMB 1 million

BYD's Blade Battery — a lithium iron phosphate (LFP) cell-to-pack technology — provided competitive advantages in safety, longevity, and cost. BYD also supplied Blade Batteries to Tesla for certain Model Y variants.

BYD launched the "Ocean" and "Dynasty" design families with distinct aesthetics, strengthening brand identity and enabling clearer market segmentation. Designer Wolfgang Egger, former head of design at Audi and Alfa Romeo, joined BYD to elevate premium model design.

GLOBAL EXPANSION

BYD began significant international expansion in 2023:
- Europe: Sales launched in Norway, Sweden, Germany, France, Netherlands, UK, Belgium. BYD established a European headquarters in Amsterdam. The Atto 3, Seal, and Han were the initial models. Market reception was positive in Norway and the Netherlands, more mixed in Germany due to brand unfamiliarity and dealer network limitations.
- Southeast Asia: BYD became the top-selling EV brand in Thailand. The company announced a manufacturing plant in Thailand, its first production facility outside China, with capacity of 150,000 vehicles per year.
- Australia: Launched with Atto 3 and Seal, gaining meaningful market share in a market with no domestic production.
- Brazil: Announced manufacturing partnership with Caoa, a local assembler, targeting production of three models in Bahia state beginning in 2025.
- Latin America: Sales launched in multiple markets; BYD became the top EV brand in several Latin American countries.

2024: EUROPEAN TARIFFS, GLOBAL MANUFACTURING RESPONSE

The European Commission's anti-subsidy investigation and provisional tariffs (announced June 2024) forced BYD to accelerate its European manufacturing strategy. BYD had already announced a manufacturing plant in Hungary (Szeged) in December 2023. In 2024, this investment was confirmed at €4 billion, with production planned for late 2025 or 2026.

The EU tariffs — BYD received a relatively favorable rate of approximately 17% additional duty versus the existing 10%, compared to higher rates for SAIC and Geely — still increased price pressure. BYD communicated that European manufacturing would address tariff concerns for the long term.

In the US market, BYD did not sell passenger vehicles due to the 100% tariff imposed by the Biden administration (later maintained by the Trump administration). BYD explored commercial vehicle sales and considered potential manufacturing options for longer-term US market entry.

BYD launched the DM5 plug-in hybrid system, capable of over 2,000 km range on a full tank and battery charge. The system dramatically improved the value proposition of PHEVs by eliminating range anxiety at a lower total cost than pure BEVs.

BYD's Seagull was launched in multiple international markets at significantly higher prices than the Chinese domestic price due to shipping costs, import duties, and dealer margins, but remained highly competitive versus local alternatives.

BYD Semiconductor, the company's in-house chip subsidiary, accelerated development of IGBT and SiC power semiconductors, reducing external procurement costs and supply vulnerability. BYD aimed for full self-sufficiency in power semiconductors for its own vehicles by 2026.

2025: COMPETITION INTENSIFIES, LUXURY PUSH

BYD faced intensifying domestic competition in China from Huawei-backed Aito, Xiaomi's SU7, Li Auto's extended-range models, and aggressive pricing moves by Tesla. The company responded with frequent model updates and price adjustments.

Internationally, BYD's European sales grew but remained below management expectations due to slow charging infrastructure development, dealer network limitations, and consumer concerns about after-sales service. The company invested heavily in establishing branded service networks in key European markets.

BYD's luxury Yangwang and Fang Cheng Bao brands gained domestic traction. The Yangwang U9 supercar helped build brand credibility in the ultra-premium segment.

BYD announced a strategic partnership with Uber for EV fleet deployment globally, providing vehicles to Uber's driver partners in multiple markets at preferential terms.

The company's battery business, FinDreams Battery, began supplying more external customers including Toyota (for bZ3), Ford (for certain markets), and multiple European OEMs through long-term supply agreements.

2026 OUTLOOK

BYD targeted 5 million annual NEV deliveries globally by 2026–2027. The company's Hungarian plant was expected to begin production in 2026, marking BYD's first European-manufactured vehicles. Brazilian production was expected to ramp toward 150,000 units annually.

Key strategic priorities: defending domestic China market share against new entrants, accelerating European market penetration, managing the tariff environment, and building the Denza and Yangwang premium brands globally.
